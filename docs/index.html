03 · Why the first dashboard misled

Totals Count Days, Not Demand

The original Tableau view summed trips per rain bucket. A bucket with more days always holds more trips, so the chart mostly measured how often each kind of weather happens. Taking the wettest of many stations each day also pushed days into the rain buckets.

05 · Method and data

How to Read This

The Comparison

Demand is trips per pickup day. A rainy Tuesday in March is compared only with dry Tuesdays in that same March, so season, weekday and the long-run trend (including congestion pricing from January 2025) cancel out. Snow days are compared with dry days; freezing and hot days with dry days that were not freezing or hot.

A log-linear regression with month and weekday fixed effects, run in analysis/weather_effect.py, gives the same picture and is in results.json.

The Data

    Every Number, Checkable
